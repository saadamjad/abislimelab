Terms of sale

Placing an order

Placing an order on this website creates an order request with the status Awaiting WhatsApp confirmation. It is not confirmed until our team confirms it on WhatsApp.

Payment

No payment is taken on this website. Your invoice shows the payment status as Unpaid. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp after we confirm your order, and an order is marked as paid only once we've received payment.

Prices

Prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Delivery charges are shown before you place your order. If anything on your invoice looks wrong, we'll sort it out with you on WhatsApp before confirming.

Products

Slime is handmade, so colors and textures may vary slightly from the pictures. Slime is not food. Keep it away from young children who might put it in their mouth, and keep it off fabrics and carpets.
