Shipping & delivery

Where we deliver

We deliver within Pakistan. Choose your city at checkout. If it isn't listed, pick Other and type it in.

Delivery charges

Delivery is per order. You'll always see the exact delivery charge in your cart and at checkout before you place your order.

When your order ships

Your order ships after we confirm it on WhatsApp. Send your invoice to after checkout, and we'll let you know the next steps and expected delivery there.
