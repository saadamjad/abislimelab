Our story

Made slowly,
stretched endlessly.

Abi's Slime Lab started on a kitchen table in DHA, Karachi, chasing one perfect texture. Today every jar still leaves that same studio.

A sensory studio for texture lovers

Every batch is mixed, scented and stretched by hand in small quantities: cloud, butter, clear, crunchy and icee. Five signature textures, restocked every Thursday.

How we make it

  • Mixed in micro-batches. Never more than 20 jars at a time, so every texture is checked by hand.
  • Cured, then tested. Clear slimes rest for four weeks. Every batch is stretched before it's sealed.
  • Skin-safe scents. Cosmetic-grade fragrance oils, borax-free activation, sealed airtight.

How we sell

Order online and confirm on WhatsApp. We confirm within the hour during studio hours, deliver the same day within Karachi, and ship nationwide via Leopards Courier.
