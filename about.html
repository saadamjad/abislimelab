🧪 about us

Hi, we're Abi's Slime Lab

Abi's Slime Lab is a small slime shop obsessed with texture. We mix our slimes by hand and keep playing with colors, scents and add-ins until each one feels just right.

What we make

Butter slime that spreads like frosting. Cloud slime that drizzles like snow. Clear slime for bubble pops, crunchy slime for loud clicks, glitter slime for sparkle, and DIY kits for making your own.

How we sell

We confirm every order personally on WhatsApp. That's why there's no online payment on our website. You place your order, send us your invoice, and we take it from there.
