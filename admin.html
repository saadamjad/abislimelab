How publishing works
  1. Edit products and collections below. Your work is saved as a draft in this browser.
  2. Click Download products.js and Download sitemap.xml.
  3. In Hostinger File Manager, upload products.js into public_html/data/ and sitemap.xml into public_html/, replacing the old files.
  4. Upload new product photos into public_html/assets/img/ and list them under Images, e.g. assets/img/galaxy.webp.
